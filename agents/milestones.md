# Visamp Milestones

Milestones define coherent product states. Requirements are the definition of capability; milestones define the order we are pursuing them.

## M0 — Planning reset
**Status:** complete

Replace Linear-led planning with the lightweight agent workflow in this directory and realign the roadmap around the new Visamp vision.

## M1 — Studio + marketplace prototype
**Status:** current

Prove the integrated artist workflow: My Videos → Video Maker → eligible visual → params → product licence when required → export.

**Exit:** R090-R094 are implemented and the flow is ready to put in front of real musicians and visual creators.

## M2 — Core-loop validation

Put M1 in front of real musicians and visual creators, observe use, collect structured feedback and test willingness to pay / create marketplace inventory.

**Exit:** enough behavioural evidence exists to decide what must change before expanding the product.

## M3 — Creator marketplace

Creators can independently publish, price and manage visual products; artists can discover and license them; marketplace economics and licensing are production-capable.

## M4 — Release content studio

Artists can turn a track + licensed visual into a useful set of release-ready content assets across common formats.

## M5 — Campaigns and publishing

Artists can organise releases, build campaigns, connect supported social accounts and schedule/publish content.

## M6 — Visamp network

Artists can publish eligible work into Visamp's own public discovery and viewing experience, with strong attribution to music artists and visual creators.

## M7 — Agent platform

Core Visamp capabilities are exposed through authenticated APIs/MCP so AI agents and partner products can discover and use the service programmatically.
