# Terms and content permissions (VIS-148)

The Terms and Conditions and Licencing routes render dedicated components under
`apps/web/components/site`. Version 1.0 is dated 20 September 2026.

## Owner-confirmed decisions

- Operator: VisArc, South Australia, Australia.
- Public visualisations permit playback and forks within VisAmp. External use
  requires separate permission unless another licence or law permits it.
- Public images, vectors and models retain permission for incorporation in
  exported videos shared outside VisAmp. This is not standalone redistribution
  permission and does not supply missing music or visualisation rights.
- The separately accepted hosted-music agreement remains authoritative. The
  Licencing page renders its existing summary and version directly.

## Product references

Credits and refund wording follows `dev/ai-credits-and-stripe.md`. Asset visibility
wording follows the asset library and migration `20260909020000_visual_assets.sql`:
previously published assets remain available to existing references when made
private; withdrawal is separate. Analytics consent remains separate from terms.

## Review and follow-up

These pages are drafted for owner and qualified legal review before production.
Confirm that VisArc identifies the contracting legal entity sufficiently and add
any required business identifiers/contact particulars during that review.
No new terms acceptance UI, acceptance-version storage or migration of existing
users is implemented by this content change. Posting the pages does not establish
recorded assent or retroactively license existing uploads. A separate acceptance
rollout is needed before relying on the new content grants. Existing music
onboarding acceptance is unchanged. No production release is included.
